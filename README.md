<h1>EatWhat Privacy Policy</h1>

<p>Last updated: May 2026</p>

<p>EatWhat does not require account registration and does not collect personally identifiable information.</p>

<h2>Information Collection</h2>

<p>Food data, recent history, and random selection logic are processed locally on your device. The app does not upload your food choices, usage history, or personal content to external servers.</p>

<h2>Third-Party Services</h2>

<p>The current version of the app does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or AI APIs.</p>

EatWhat Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

EatWhat does not require account registration and does not collect personally identifiable information.

Information Collection

Food data, recent history, and random selection logic are processed locally on your device. The app does not upload your food choices, usage history, or personal content to external servers.

Third-Party Services

The current version of the app does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or AI APIs.
